# Where to publish

Since 2026-11-26 the public site is www.giulioruffini.com, built from the repository `giulioruffini/giulioruffini-dot-com-site`. New essays go into its `public/blogs/` folder with an entry in `content/blog/`; the reference pages (Kolmogorov Theory, neuroscience, stimulation) live in its `content/pages/`.

This site keeps the collaboration pages under `assets/collab/`, the paper and slide files under `assets/papers/` and `assets/slides/`, and the papers chat page. Every page that moved to www.giulioruffini.com is replaced here by a small forwarding page, so old links keep working.
