I am a theoretical physicist and computational neuroscientist researching consciousness through the lens of algorithmic information theory. My work involves creating advanced biophysical models to understand brain dynamics and structured experience, and translating these insights into clinical applications—particularly in the field of noninvasive brain stimulation, where computational neuropsychiatry and computational neurophenomenology will play a key role. I’m especially interested in how these models illuminate human experience and their broader implications for artificial intelligence and other life forms. Other:
- Companies I co-founded: [Neuroelectrics](https://neuroelectrics.com) and [Starlab](https://starlab.es).  Starlab: We provide state-of-the-art neuroimaging data analysis services in four main areas: Experience, Aging, Neurodevelopment, and Neuromodulation.
We  combine Artificial Intelligence and Neuroscience to facilitate your access to medical class Neurotechnology for Electroencephalography (EEG) and Transcranial Electrical Stimulation (tES). We have created diagnostic and treatment telemedicine wireless platforms in order to reinvent the way we observe and treat the human brain. We are a team of neuroscientists, i.e. physicists, mathematicians, cognitive scientists, environmentalists, and engineers backed up by a business team aiming to translate science into breakthrough technologies with a profound and positive impact on our clients and the society in general. Pioneering deep technologies will be applied to improve people's quality of life and we will make it happen!  Neuroelectrics is a creative, high-tech company offering the best in class non-invasive and high definition electrical brain stimulation technology for personalized neuromodulation based on computational modeling of the brain. By measuring and modifying brain function, we aim to restore brain health, minimize disabilities and create a better life for patients.

- Foundation I co-founded: The Barcelona Computational Foundation  [BCOM](https://bcom.one). BCOM is a visionary non-profit research foundation dedicated to understanding and recreating life, brains, and minds as fundamentally computational phenomena. Our mission is to advance computational neuropsychiatry and neurophenomenology, pioneer neurosynthetic AI, and ultimately provide means for better lives and societies- biological, hybrid or synthetic.
