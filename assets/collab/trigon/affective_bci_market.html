Executive Summary

Reliable measurement of affective state via EEG — operationalized as continuous valence (approach/withdrawal) and arousal (activation intensity) dynamics — opens six commercially viable market segments with a combined serviceable addressable market (SAM) of approximately €6.5–12.5B by 2030.

The Trigon (Neuroelectrics + BCOM + Starlab) holds a structurally unique position in this market because all four layers of the value chain sit inside the consortium: theory and mechanistic models (BCOM — Whole-Brain Model for Mood Disorders, predictive-coding framework, ALGA Engine), affective signal software (Starlab — EmoWave valence/arousal pipeline + curated EEG corpus), clinical-grade hardware and closed-loop stimulation (NE — Enobio, Starstim, Neurotwin), and regulatory, sponsor, and route-to-market (NE — ISO 13485, MDR/FDA, SaaS productisation). This read + interpret + write + ship stack is not replicated by any single competitor.

The Trigon's affective story is also not purely EEG. Three multimodal capabilities — principled quantification of subjective experience via Kokoro, per-individual Whole-Brain Models calibrated jointly from EEG and Kokoro, and foundational EEG models jointly built by Starlab and BCOM — extend the moat from "we measure a signal" to "we measure brain, mind, and behaviour in one personalised stack."

Core Strategic Thesis

The moat is not "we can measure emotion" — that capability is commoditising. The moat is a clinical-grade signal interpreted through a mechanistic brain model and a principled phenomenology layer, looped back via stimulation, and shipped through the right commercial channel. The Trigon is the only consortium in Europe that owns every link of that chain across two commercialisation lanes — clinical and non-clinical — fed by a single non-profit deep-tech source. Each entity is replaceable individually; the integrated Trigon is not.

What Each Entity Brings to Affective BCI

The operating-model split above shapes how affective BCI plays out. BCOM produces the models, Starlab develops EEG applications and runs the non-clinical channel, NE implements clinical products and supplies hardware to both. Below, the affective-BCI-specific artefacts each entity contributes.

BCOM · deep tech

Models, theory, phenomenology.
  • Whole-Brain Model for Mood Disorders — interpretive layer connecting valence/arousal dynamics to mechanism (predictive coding, plasticity windows, depressive attractors). Personalised per individual.
  • Kokoro — principled quantification of subjective experience. Structured phenomenological interviews → comparable, longitudinal, machine-readable variables. A measurement layer that complements EEG.
  • Algorithmic-agent and emotion theory — Kolmogorov Theory, predictive coding, the ALGA Engine for affect-modulating generative content.
  • Archeon — literature-grounded archeograph for the affective-neuroscience evidence base.

Starlab · EEG apps + non-clinical channel

Develops the EEG software; sells non-clinical.
  • EmoWave — EEG valence/arousal software; the canonical affective output downstream products consume. Starlab's flagship non-clinical commercial product.
  • EEG application development — pipelines, biomarker extraction, artefact handling, normative comparison. Licensed to NE for clinical use.
  • Curated EEG corpus — the normative database that gives the Trigon a unique training and validation asset (and the substrate for foundational EEG models, co-owned with BCOM).
  • Non-clinical commercialisation — direct channel into cosmetics, fragrance, neuromarketing, content, and research-services clients.

Neuroelectrics · hardware + clinical channel

Hardware everywhere; clinical implementation.
  • Enobio + Starstim — CE-marked / FDA-cleared EEG and tES hardware. The substrate every Trigon product runs on, clinical or non-clinical.
  • Clinical implementation of BCOM's WBM and personalisation (Neurotwin) and Starlab's EEG pipelines, packaged as regulatory-grade SaaS.
  • Clinical commercialisation — Class IIa+ medical software, hospital and pharma channels, MDR/FDA pathway, ISO 13485 QMS, trial sponsorship.
  • Hardware supply to Starlab's non-clinical lines — bringing clinical-grade signal quality to consumer-facing applications.

Three joint capabilities that go beyond EEG alone

Each entity is strong on its own, but the assets that no competitor can replicate are the ones that only exist when two or three of them combine — and they extend the Trigon's affective offering well past pure EEG measurement.

Kokoro · the experience layer of every application

Owned by BCOM · embedded in the apps themselves
  • At home: daily Kokoro check-in next to the home EEG session — turns "how are you feeling" into a quantified vector the clinician dashboard plots over time.
  • In clinic: in-session structured phenomenology for CBT, exposure, EMDR, and psychedelic-assisted therapy — including the moments where the patient's reported state diverges from the EEG.
  • In trials: a quantified subjective endpoint that pairs with the EEG biomarker for pharma sponsors — far better signal-to-noise than weekly PHQ-9 / HAM-D.
  • In consumer studies (non-clinical): the same instrument captures fragrance hedonics, content response, qualitative consumer insight — structured rather than free-text.
  • Result: the Trigon ships every application as EEG + Kokoro, not just EEG — a measurement instrument competitors don't even know how to spec.

Personalised Whole-Brain Models

Owned by BCOM · implemented by NE (clinical) and SL (non-clinical)
  • Per-individual brain models calibrated jointly from EEG (Starlab pipelines) and Kokoro (BCOM) — fitted to neural dynamics and the patient's reported experience.
  • Clinical implementation (NE): the natural evolution of Neurotwin — from "personalised tDCS dose" to "personalised affective model" driving response prediction and closed-loop stimulation.
  • Non-clinical implementation (Starlab): participant-specific affective baselines for premium cosmetics, fragrance, and content studies.

Foundational EEG Models

Joint SL × BCOM project · consumed by both lanes
  • Self-supervised pretraining on Starlab's curated EEG corpus, with BCOM's mechanistic priors (Whole-Brain Model dynamics, predictive-coding objectives) baked into architecture and training.
  • Single shared backbone: EmoWave (Starlab non-clinical) and NE's clinical SaaS both fine-tune heads off the same foundation.
  • Strategic IP for the foundation-model era — a defensible asset competitors without a corpus or theoretical priors simply cannot build. Part of BCOM's deep-tech contribution to the consortium.

Segment Analysis

1. Clinical Home Monitoring — Psychiatric Remote Patient Monitoring

Clinical · NE-led Primary Opportunity
SAM 2030 €2.5–5B
Revenue Model €50–150 per patient / month
Time to Revenue 18–30 mo
Regulatory Class IIa monitoring only

The opportunity: Psychiatric care relies on sparse, subjective self-reports (PHQ-9, HAM-D) collected every 4–8 weeks. Continuous remote monitoring — EEG and a structured phenomenological companion — transforms this into dense, longitudinal data on both brain state and lived experience, enabling early intervention, medication titration, and prodromal detection.

What we sell: Daily 5-minute standardised EEG assessment at home (resting state + brief affective provocation) paired with a daily Kokoro phenomenological check-in — a few minutes of structured first-person reporting that turns "how do you feel?" into a quantified vector comparable across days, patients, and conditions. Both feed a clinician dashboard showing affective baseline trajectories, medication response curves, and divergence between physiological state and reported state — often the earliest warning of relapse.

Who pays: Health systems and payers. Value proposition: reduced hospitalisation from early intervention, fewer medication trial-and-error cycles, fewer crisis ER presentations.

Why the Trigon wins:

Enobio + Starstim NE Neurotwin · MDR/FDA NE EmoWave + EEG corpus SL Whole-Brain Model · ALGA BCOM

This is the natural wedge into the closed-loop digital therapeutic: start with EmoWave-driven monitoring (lower regulatory burden, Class IIa), build the longitudinal dataset interpreted through BCOM's Whole-Brain Model, then layer in Starstim-based stimulation (Class IIb/III) once the evidence base and clearance are in place.

2. Pharma Endpoint-as-a-Service

Clinical · NE-led Primary Opportunity
SAM 2030 €1.5–3B
Revenue Model €200–500K per trial engagement
Time to Revenue 12–24 mo
Regulatory Moderate endpoint qualification

The opportunity: MDD/anxiety clinical trials fail at high rates partly because primary endpoints are subjective, noisy, and sparse. The Trigon's offer is a paired endpoint — objective EEG-derived affective biomarkers (EmoWave + WBM-MD interpretation) and structured subjective outcomes (Kokoro) — collected daily instead of every visit. Together they substantially improve signal-to-noise and can potentially halve required enrollment.

What we sell: A managed data service to CROs and pharma sponsors: validated hardware, standardised EEG protocols, Kokoro-based structured-experience capture, cloud analytics platform, regulatory-ready data packages. Not the device — the endpoint pair.

Who pays: Pharma companies and CROs. Halving enrollment in a Phase II/III trial saves tens of millions per program.

Why the Trigon wins:

Mechanistic biomarker (WBM-MD) BCOM EmoWave endpoint pipeline SL Trial sponsorship · MDR/FDA NE Existing trial network NE

Fastest path to meaningful revenue. NE's existing trial relationships (ADASTRA, SYNERGIA) give immediate warm introductions; BCOM's Whole-Brain Model for Mood Disorders provides the mechanistic story that turns EmoWave outputs into a defensible endpoint, not just a metric.

3. Cosmetics & Fragrance Testing

Non-clinical · Starlab-led Strategic Growth
SAM 2030 €1–2B
Revenue Model €100–300K annual license / client
Time to Revenue 6–12 mo
Regulatory Minimal non-medical device

The opportunity: The gap between what consumers say and what they feel is widest in sensory products. Hedonic response to fragrance, texture, and packaging is pre-verbal — EEG captures the first 500ms that self-report destroys.

Key applications:

  • Fragrance development: Real-time valence/arousal trajectories across dry-down phases (top, heart, base notes). Objective data for perfumers replacing trained verbal panels.
  • Texture and haptics: Emotional response to cream feel, compact snap, bottle weight. Luxury brands obsess over this and currently cannot measure it.
  • Claim substantiation: "Clinically proven to improve wellbeing" operationalized as a measurable affective EEG shift vs. control. Defensible marketing claims for premium products.
  • Packaging: 200–400ms emotional first-impression response before conscious evaluation — where impulse purchase decisions are actually made.

Who pays: L'Oréal, Estée Lauder, Givaudan, Firmenich, IFF — companies with R&D budgets in the hundreds of millions and high margins to absorb premium tooling. Target: 50–100 global brands and top 20 agencies.

EmoWave (the product) SL Normative EEG corpus SL Enobio hardware + QMS NE Calibration / dossier BCOM

Strategic role: Fastest time-to-revenue, lowest regulatory burden. EmoWave is essentially the product here; NE supplies the clinical-grade substrate that justifies premium pricing vs. consumer-grade competitors; BCOM provides the validation dossier. Cash-generating beachhead that funds the clinical plays — and the first place Starlab's affective-computing services can be packaged into a repeatable offering.

4. Neuromarketing Platform (Broad)

Non-clinical · Starlab-led Growth Market
SAM 2030 €0.8–1.2B
Revenue Model Hardware + SaaS per-study analytics
Time to Revenue 6–12 mo
Regulatory None

The opportunity: Ad testing, UX research, shelf layout optimization, content testing for studios and streaming platforms. Existing market (~€1B) currently served by firms using consumer-grade hardware with poorly validated metrics.

Trigon differentiation: Don't sell the study — sell the platform. EmoWave delivers the affective metric, the BCOM-curated normative database makes it interpretable, and NE supplies the clinical-grade hardware and validation paperwork. Enterprise consumer-insights teams (Unilever, P&G, Netflix) can run studies themselves on a platform that survives scientific scrutiny.

Revenue model: Hardware lease (NE) + per-study EmoWave SaaS analytics licence (SL) + normative database access (SL+BCOM). Recurring, not project-based.

EmoWave + protocol SL Clinical-grade hardware NE Validation dossier BCOM

Risk: More competitive than other segments. Consumer-grade headbands (Emotiv, Muse) are "good enough" for many buyers. The Trigon must compete on validation and credibility, not just signal quality — which is precisely the layer BCOM's dossier and NE's clinical pedigree bring.

5. Psychotherapy Augmentation

Clinical · NE-led Emerging
SAM 2030 €0.5–1B
Revenue Model €200–400 per clinician / month
Time to Revenue 24–36 mo
Regulatory Class I–IIa

Three plays — all built around the EEG + Kokoro pairing:

  • In-session therapist dashboard: Real-time patient affective state during CBT, exposure therapy, EMDR, or psychedelic-assisted therapy. Detects when verbal report diverges from physiological state ("I'm fine" + high arousal/negative valence). Kokoro adds a structured, prompt-driven phenomenology layer the therapist can review in seconds.
  • Between-session outcome tracking: Brief daily EEG assessment at home + a Kokoro check-in. Together they produce a longitudinal "brain × experience" record that replaces weekly self-report questionnaires (PHQ-9, GAD-7) with something far richer.
  • Psychedelic-assisted therapy outcomes: Where phenomenology is half the clinical signal, Kokoro becomes the principled measurement layer — turning subjective trip-report content into structured, comparable, longitudinal data the field currently lacks.

Who pays: Behavioural health clinics, employee assistance programmes, teletherapy platforms, psychedelic-therapy clinics.

Enobio Home + dashboard NE EmoWave session feed SL Kokoro phenomenology BCOM

Strategic note: Better positioned as a feature of the home monitoring platform (Segment 1) than a standalone product. Therapist adoption is slow and workflow-sensitive. Where the Trigon adds singular value is the combination of EmoWave's objective affect read-out with BCOM's Kokoro phenomenology layer — capturing what the patient reports alongside what the EEG says, then flagging divergence.

6. Objective Pain Assessment

Clinical · NE-led Emerging — High Ceiling
SAM 2030 €0.2–0.3B TAM far larger if validated
Revenue Model TBD device + service
Time to Revenue 36–60 mo
Regulatory Class II–III

The opportunity: Pain is universally self-reported. Objective EEG-based pain measurement would transform anesthesiology, chronic pain management, neonatal care, and analgesic drug development. The opioid crisis has made this politically urgent.

The challenge: Scientific validation is still early-stage. Decomposing affective EEG into pain-specific vs. general negative-affect components is an open research problem. Near-term SAM is small, but if validated, the TAM is enormous (€10B+ across pain management, insurance, pharma).

Pain decomposition (theory) BCOM EmoWave + pain pipeline SL Read + Write + regulatory NE

Recommendation: Fund as a Trigon research initiative — BCOM leads the predictive-coding decomposition of pain vs. general negative-affect, Starlab adapts EmoWave's pipeline to the pain context, NE stands by for the clinical-validation phase. Monitor academic progress; be ready to accelerate if validation milestones are hit.

Recommended Sequencing

The two lanes run in parallel. Starlab's non-clinical lane (EmoWave in cosmetics/neuromarketing) generates revenue fast and funds the Trigon. NE's clinical lane (pharma endpoints → home monitoring → DTx), anchored by BCOM's Whole-Brain Model and progressively absorbing Kokoro and the foundation model, builds the evidence base and the route to reimbursement. The ENAKD/Tx-style closed-loop digital therapeutic — Enobio + Starstim + EmoWave + ALGA + Kokoro under one CE mark — is the clinical endgame.

Now → 12 months

Non-clinical (Starlab-led): Cosmetics/fragrance testing on EmoWave running on NE hardware — fastest to revenue, lowest regulatory burden. Cash-generating beachhead. Clinical (NE-led): begin a pharma endpoint pilot inside an existing Trigon trial (ADASTRA / SYNERGIA), with BCOM defining the biomarker rationale.

12 → 30 months

Non-clinical (Starlab-led): EmoWave expanded into neuromarketing platform deals; first Kokoro integration in premium fragrance/content engagements. Clinical (NE-led): pharma endpoint-as-a-service at scale — 2–3 CRO partnerships validating paired EmoWave + Kokoro endpoints alongside BCOM's WBM-MD biomarker in MDD trials. Clinical home-monitoring pilot — Enobio Home with the Kokoro at-home companion app delivered to 2–3 psychiatric clinics.

30 → 60 months

Joint: first-generation foundational EEG model (SL × BCOM) shipping into both lanes — EmoWave on the non-clinical side, clinical SaaS on the NE side. Clinical (NE-led): home monitoring at scale, Class IIa clearance, payer reimbursement codes; longitudinal dataset interpreted by BCOM's WBM becomes the moat. Layer in psychotherapy-augmentation features (EmoWave + Kokoro) as part of the monitoring platform.

60+ months

Clinical endgame (NE-led): closed-loop digital therapeutic — Enobio + Starstim + EmoWave + ALGA Engine + personalised WBM under FDA De Novo or EU MDR Class IIb. A prescription, reimbursable, personalised affective intervention device — the ENAKD/Tx pattern, productised. Only achievable because the earlier phases built the data (SL), the science (BCOM), and the regulatory wrapper (NE) across all three entities.

Competitive Positioning

No competitor holds the complete theory + signal-software + phenomenology + personalised brain model + clinical-grade device + regulatory stack. EmoWave gives the Trigon an affective software layer that the device companies don't have; Enobio/Starstim/Neurotwin give it a clinical-grade hardware + stimulation layer that the consumer EEG companies don't have; BCOM's Whole-Brain Model and Kokoro give it two interpretation layers — mechanistic and phenomenological — that nobody else has at all; and the foundational EEG model trained on Starlab's corpus turns those advantages into a compounding asset.
