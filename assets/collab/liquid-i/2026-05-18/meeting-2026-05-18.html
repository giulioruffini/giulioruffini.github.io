Project narrative

Ricard pushed to sharpen the pitch. Convergence:

  • The project is a science of agents and hyper-agency — how systems of agents become agents themselves — framed around persistence of patterns rather than classical natural selection.
  • Persistence is the algorithmic primitive (mutual algorithmic information of a pattern with itself across time). Agents are one kind of persistent pattern; Gaia is another (persistent, made of agents, doesn't reproduce).
  • Co-evolution is reframed: agents that modify their environment, with the environment itself becoming an agent — a computational ecology. Lamarckian-style: agents learn within lifetime, not just mutate.
  • Tele-homeostasis as the unifying drive: agents persist by serving a higher-level pattern (kin / species); pathology = misalignment between the agent's implementation and that drive — basis for the computational neuropsychiatry leg.
